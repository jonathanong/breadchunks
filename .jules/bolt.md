## 2024-05-18 - `captures_iter` vs `find_iter` in Regex
**Learning:** `Regex::captures_iter` has significant overhead compared to `Regex::find_iter` when you just need the matched text and can derive sub-captures using simple string manipulation. In `breadchunks`, switching a header regex loop from `captures_iter` to `find_iter` reduced time spent matching headers by ~3.5x.
**Action:** When a regex capture group is just stripping a predictable prefix/suffix (like an optional leading newline), use `find_iter` and slice the resulting `&str` instead of paying the overhead for full capture groups.
