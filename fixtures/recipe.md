## Lemon Herb Roast Chicken

A simple weeknight roast chicken with bright lemon and fresh herbs. Serves 4.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (about 1.5 kg)
- 2 lemons, zested and halved
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

## Equipment

- Roasting pan or cast-iron skillet
- Kitchen twine
- Instant-read thermometer

## Method

### Prep

Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F). Pat the chicken dry with paper towels — this is the single most important step for crispy skin.

Mix the olive oil, lemon zest, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.

### Season

Rub the herb mixture all over the chicken, including under the skin over the breast meat. Stuff the cavity with the garlic and lemon halves.

Truss the legs loosely with kitchen twine so the bird holds its shape.

### Roast

Place the chicken breast-side up in the roasting pan. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes to get the skin started, then reduce to 190°C (375°F).

Continue roasting until a thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads 74°C (165°F). This takes roughly 1 hour total for a 1.5 kg bird.

### Rest

Remove from the oven and let the chicken rest uncovered for 10 minutes before carving. Resting allows the juices to redistribute.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with roasted vegetables or a simple green salad. The pan drippings make an excellent quick sauce — deglaze with a splash of white wine and a knob of butter.

## Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat gently in the oven at 160°C to preserve moisture.
