# Taxonomy of Knowledge

A fictional taxonomy used to exercise deep header hierarchies (h1 through h6).

## Natural Sciences

The natural sciences seek to understand the physical world through observation and experiment.

### Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms.

#### Zoology

Zoology focuses on animals.

##### Vertebrates

Vertebrates possess a backbone.

###### Mammals

Mammals are warm-blooded vertebrates that nurse their young with milk.

###### Birds

Birds are feathered, winged, egg-laying vertebrates.

##### Invertebrates

Invertebrates lack a backbone and represent the vast majority of animal species.

#### Botany

Botany is the study of plants, including their physiology, structure, genetics, and ecology.

### Chemistry

Chemistry examines the composition and transformation of matter.

#### Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry focuses on carbon-containing compounds.

#### Inorganic Chemistry

Inorganic chemistry covers compounds that are not primarily carbon-based.

## Formal Sciences

The formal sciences develop formal systems such as logic and mathematics.

### Mathematics

Mathematics is the language of patterns and structure.

#### Algebra

Algebra studies mathematical symbols and the rules for manipulating them.

#### Calculus

Calculus deals with continuous change through derivatives and integrals.

### Logic

Logic provides the principles of valid inference and correct reasoning.

#### Deductive Reasoning

Deductive reasoning draws conclusions that necessarily follow from premises.

#### Inductive Reasoning

Inductive reasoning draws general conclusions from specific observations.
